**Guitar Tablature Estimation from Audio with CNN and CRNN Models**

CS229 Final Project  
Team members: replace with author names and SUNet IDs  
Term: replace with the course offering used for the submission

This report studies automatic guitar tablature estimation from audio using the `tab-cnn` codebase, which is built around the TabCNN formulation and extended here with a convolutional recurrent variant. The project evaluates whether adding temporal modeling improves frame-level transcription quality on GuitarSet under guitarist-held-out cross-validation.
